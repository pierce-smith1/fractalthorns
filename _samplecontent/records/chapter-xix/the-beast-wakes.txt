_ :

The vast and silent universe sleeps.

The stars hang fixed in constellations, sprawled in sheets of glittering light flung across the darkness. Clouds of chromatic dust billow into nebulae, droplets of paint falling into the darkest ocean, their gradual, shimmering descent frozen in time, drenched in cosmic colors of mystical vibrance and purity. The arms of galaxies reach for the edges of the cosmos, the shadow of a trillion candles floating in the night. Once, the great dark beyond had no edges to even reach for, only points at infinity. But now, the fractal wall encroaches, condensing infinity into a writhing landslide of impossible shapes, whose unknowable borders crash closer and closer towards the end of everything. An occasional streak of white light across the stars marks the gradual evacuation of the NYXITE.

Pinned in the center of it all are the planets, those little, inconsequential, infinitesimal balls of rock and ice, the sons and daughters of stars that were once stepping stones for the NYXITE as they hopped from world to world, gorging only their curiosity, assimilating exploration and experimentation into progress and power. The scale of everything once inspired awe - an awe that renews as they gaze into the eyes of the abyss.

Here, on one of these ancient, forgotten worlds, peeking sheepishly over a blanket of clouds as night falls into calming blues over an alien field, a silent observatory trains its telescopic eye not on the wall, but on a shooting star, streaking with unholy crimson light across the backdrop of the cosmos. Its gaze is not alone. The NYXITE know what it is.

BEAST sails in the dark ocean, trailed by a river of flowing fuschia that reflects the Hyperverse itself. Its construction is made of cracked, onyx stones, drenched in otherworldly maroon, glowing with the wondrous pure light of the bright galaxies all around it. Its form is taken after some four-legged mammal in a reality of the distant past, though the inspiration is loose - its head is a pointed, eyeless cage of red flame; its legs are massively disproportionate, the front ones ending in points and being threefold longer and bulkier than the hind legs, which twist in short, stubby segments that curl close to its body; and its "tail" is more akin to the fins of a whale, a flat, sprawling mesh cut from a space-filling curve. Its chest opens up to another core of red flame, a set of dark ribs containing its churning, ascendant essence.

As it streaks across the night, the construct begins to gently slow, the stars racing past it shrinking from streaks of light back into mere points. It comes to rest in the orbit of an ice giant wrapped in swirling clouds of deep cyan. BEAST floats in the shadow of the planet's grandeur, innocently, like a leaf on the surface of a pond, watching the great NYXITE cities spin with the enormous planet below. Great citadels of dark purple steel blink with life as they slice through the atmosphere, and rows of enormous, interlocking cubic buildings line the bands of latitude, resembling a cliffside dwelling on a planetary scale. It is a pearl of nature utterly conquered by civilization, pumping with the lifeblood of activity as tiny vehicles hop to and fro above its surface, fervent yet morose in their preparations to abandon the world.

BEAST simply watches, its limbs still, submerged in the depths of space. And as it does, just underneath the pole of the planet, a skeleton of ivory begins to materialize. Five lines, razor thin against the scale of the planet, radiate outward from a point. With great precision and terrifying speed, they join together into triangles, whose faces fill with glassy crimson - and the structure grows, the triangles tessellating into an icosahedron, swallowing the entire world, a quadrillion times larger than the tiny construct silently orchestrating its very construction. 

Soon it is utterly entrapped. The faces of the shape slowly frost over, tiny fractals cracking across the surface as time within grows colder and colder. The world within slows down, then falls slower still, and slower still, until it has stopped entirely, and is lost behind the icosahedron's now opaque facade. It is over. A population of hundreds of billions, and a world tens of thousands of years old,  built with more matter, mechanics, and math than most species will move in the entire history of their existence, is bottled into such an unceremonious capsule. Like an imploding star, the great icosahedron silently collapses, sending reverberations through the lattice of reality itself, condensing until is just the size of a marble, shining like a tiny star, rising miniature pillars of vermilion light out into space. It flies somewhere unknown - a little seed that can be safely stashed away in the Hyperverse, away from the rotting fruits of this world's negligence. 

It is then that a column of righteous azure flames slams into BEAST from above. The unsuspecting construct is limply flung across the plane of the solar system by the sudden attack, its hallowed stone licked with searing plasma. Tracing the flames to their source, the silhouette of a great 10-pointed star looms above, a frame of dark steel that covers a humming magenta core, blotting out the galaxies above as a shadow of vengeance. It stands strongly and firmly in space, as all around it, lightyears away, its brethren flee from the encroaching wall.

The pieces of the star's frame shift and sharply reconfigure in a choreography of interlocking mechanisms, playing like shadows against a violet sun, loading and unveiling another set of weapons. A battalion of cannons along the perimeter of the star are set alight, lashing thunderous bolts of white light across the cosmos as they fire, sending a flurry of electrified spears towards BEAST. The projectiles arc with deadly precision across the bounds of space, slamming into the construct's impervious carparace and unleashing a paralyzing cascade of voltage. BEAST is, of course, unhurt, but lies disoriented for a moment, its senses confused by the rush of stimulus. Calmly, the faces of its polygonal body pulse and become ephemeral for a moment, shards of black rock melting apart from one another, freeing the construct from the grasp of the spears - only for a moment, until they stubbornly whir back to life and reattach, again overloading BEAST's mind with enraging noise.

It attempts to focus, and begins to grasp the NYXITE starship in the claws of another icosahedral prison, hoping to add this stubborn assailant to its archive. But the NYXITE are prepared. The warship's frame reconfigures again, miles of crystalline steel sliding across its glowing core and locking into new positions, one that forms concentric stars around the center of the ship, exposing a small rhombic generator connected to the arms of the frame by hundreds of glowing cables. The generator blinks, quickly dimming the ship's luminescent surfaces until it fades fully into the background of space... and BEAST's icosahedron now entraps nothing but the void, as the starship's shadow reappears thousands of kilometers to the side. Its generator spins promptly to life again, pulling the frame of the ship back together into combat formation.

Under the unwavering pressure of the starship, the construct's synthetic mind races only with rage now. The polygonal skin on its massive front legs begins to crack apart, splitting the limbs open to reveal massive blades of fallen light, seething with crimson malice. Swiftly it slices through space, cutting each of the spears that assault it in half, fully disarming them as it sets them alight with deep red flame. A flurry of replacements is launched, whizzing through the void behind the construct - but before they can collide with its body again, a shield of impossible blackness crystallizes between BEAST and the warship, and the hapless projectiles become stuck in pits of space where time is completely lost, frozen to an impossible standstill, invisible in the blackness as no longer can even photons reach them.

A symphony of harmonic mechanisms plays as the starship reconfigures once more, exposing a pentagonal mouth that blazes with charged plasma. BEAST zips across space with unreal speed, a red streak slicing the cosmos in half, attempting to close the distance in the blink of an eye, its blades trembling with hunger - yet the hunter does not blink, and the NYXITE land a second pillar of plasma straight against BEAST's chest. It is flung off course, and the warship's frame slides with calculated haste, training the cannon for another shot. BEAST is able to just barely gather itself, in the few seconds between the cannon's pulses, and conjure another wall of stopped time, catching a searing column of plasma in a trap of infinity before it can be hit again.

With the cannon now exhausted for a few brief moments, The construct swings above the starship, and more splinters of dark space materialize before it, spinning quickly to orient themselves towards the NYXITE. With a swing of its bladed arms, it throws them in an octagonal spread towards the starship. As the void spikes rip through spacetime, they emit blinding sparks of light - then they intersect the frame of the ship, perfectly disassembling the hull as space collapses in their wake, leaving long chasms of ravaged metal in the once-whole body of the ship. The arms of the star are cut - entire sections now only hang on by minuscule beams, and fragments of its sparking core are sundered across space, crushed into scintillating dust that arcs with bleeding electricity. The construction of hull is, of course, armored to the peak of NYXITE ability, but there is no material that could withstand power that transcends physicality itself. 

The ship, now ostensibly in the throes of a panic, reconfigures once more, its once rigid form limping with a newfound struggle. The rhombic core at its heart is once again exposed, its newly sliced arteries bleeding into space, and seems to blink in preparation of another warp - or something worse. BEAST glares at the pulsing geometry with bared teeth. It aims a wrathful splinter of frozen space directly at the core, tensing with rage, a crossbow's bolt pulled back with the weight of a planet, and swings viciously to send it on its way.

The heart of the ship is struck in nearly an instant. Effortlessly, the spike atomizes its prey in a cosmic cerulean flash. The ship shakes for a moment, a horrible shockwave rattling through its steel halls, then begins its fall. Hundreds of steel limbs come slowly unlinked, now floating loosely in the embrace of the void as their body grows cold. The glow from the reactor, and its illuminant blood that flowed and drenched the arms of the craft with color, drains away into the inky blackness. BEAST gorges its rage on the vista of destruction - it is the ironic image of an entire civilization laid to waste, a pathetic pile of bones scattered, sinking, in the depths of the ocean. 

The shattered pieces of the core - half still in their orderly polyhedric form, and half a jagged, torn mesh - float apart into the arms of space, little sparks of stubborn life arcing gently between them. But then, a small, rising hum begins to grow from within each. The compressed matter inside begins to churn and bubble as a runaway reaction explodes deep within the molecular lattice - two, then four, then eight, then sixteen of their atoms rapidly rearrange and disassemble, swelling with chaotic energy. BEAST is simply unaware. 

It does not know the craft was not killed, it was sacrificed.  

The calm reverie of the stars is interrupted by an apocalyptic strike of lightning. The power of a supergiant's supernova bursts without warning or recourse, pulverizing the remains of the ship with an inferno of plasma, and soon crashing equally over BEAST, pushing it into a backwards tumble through a sea of chromatic fire. Though utterly submerged in the depths of the explosion, it is, as always, unhurt, and the construct is able to quickly compose itself, right its orientation, and frost over a sheet of space between it and the source, catching a ray of the blast in a prison of time, leaving it atop a tiny island of calm in a sea of rage. The construct then floats defiantly in the eye of a catastrophic storm, a typhoon of boundless, vibrant energy roaring past in every direction, so ferocious that reality itself seems to struggle depicting it.

Out of BEAST's sight, the exploding core continues to grow, hotter, brighter, larger... until the plasma no longer races from it, but slows, and slows, and begins to collapse inwards, birthing a tiny, synthetic, ravenously expanding star. BEAST, still floating behind its shield of time, is granted a moment of peace as the explosion fades. Its eyeless head watches in curiosity as the waves of the supernova pass in a monumental sphere around it, seeing the storm billow out into the free plains of the beyond, engulfing the planets, engulfing the stars, the nebulae and the dark background of the void in clouds of chaotic magic, as if it is watching the birth of a new universe from its very center.

Just behind BEAST, the ghost of the starship screams. Its brief life of undeath as an artificial star has ended. The star has, in so few moments, grown so massive and so dense it has collapsed into something new, a singularity churning with fiery darkness that feeds off the boiled remains of its old body. Before BEAST even realizes the unstoppable grasp of gravity has found its body, it is too late. Its tail has already been pulled far enough that it has fallen into the dark scar of time, the shield that once protected it - and now the construct is sucked under the waves. It tries to detach its tail and fling itself away, but the event horizon, swirling with vengeant malice, has its target hopelessly ensnared. As it is pulled further in, its legs become caught in time, their unbreakable stones being torn apart and siphoned away with the construct's silent and unheard screams. Slowly and horrifically, BEAST's entire body is pulled by an unstoppable force through an immovable object, disappearing into the unseen veil of another dimension; the flames of its essence are snuffed, the darkness all-consuming, until it is gone, destroyed, forgotten, a scattered mosaic of molecules trapped in the span of nowhere for all the unmeasurable continuum of time.

EMBER's rage is doused. 

A pacific sea of clouds sails across the soft gusts of wind. The observatory's shining pupil, reflecting the last few drops of sunlight as dusk falls gently below the deep blue horizon, gazes up at a tiny point of light, a new point, twinkling brightly among the calm constellations. Inside the grand, empty dome of the observatory's geodesic eyelids, a tiny machine clicks, taking a photograph, and automatically filing a new astronomical observation, printing another page of dusty paper onto a near-overflowing stack.

Then, the eye cautiously moves back to the wall.
